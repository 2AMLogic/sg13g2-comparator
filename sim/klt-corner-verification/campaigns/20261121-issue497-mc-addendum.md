# Interpretation of the issue #497 stored noise screens

This addendum qualifies the conclusion in the [original stored-evidence
summary](20261121-issue497-mc.md). All three measured settings missed the
1.0 mV rms screen at 36.27 nV/√Hz. At 1, 2 and 4 fF per side the 18-point
means were 1.0411, 1.0218 and 1.0249 mV rms. The corresponding worst Row 3c
delays were 1.8804, 1.8988 and 1.9364 ns, all within 2.0 ns.

The paired standard errors against the control were 29–36 µV. These data
support eliminating the three tested settings. Saturation and the behavior
at other capacitances remain undetermined. Full-grid Rows 1–5 were unmeasured
for all three settings.

The [revised table](20261121-issue497/capacitance-r2.md) and
[JSON report](20261121-issue497/capacitance-r2.json) use the archived
envelopes. The [dated analysis](../../comparator-integration-capacitance/reports/20261121-issue497.md)
describes the calibration and sampling uncertainty.
