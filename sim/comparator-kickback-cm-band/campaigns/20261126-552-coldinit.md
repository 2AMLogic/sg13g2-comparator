# Negative result: behavioural current-source initialization collapsed at every ss/-40 point (issue #552, attempt 1)

The first fleet attempt at the issue #552 cold-edge measurement drove the
four floating input pins with self-referencing behavioural current sources
(`Binit_bpp bpp 0 i = 'v(clti)*0.01*(v(bsp)-v(bpp))'` and its three
siblings, each controlled by the release pulse) and
the three latch outputs with switches to 0 V. The design goal was the same
as the second attempt: hold the pins at their analytic DC values,
hold the outputs at the wrong-polarity LOW rail, release before the 29 ns
baseline. The pin drive is released by multiplication (a 0 A current source
is an open circuit), which is why it was tried first.

**Outcome: deterministic hard failure at all four points.** ngspice
aborted every transient with `timestep too small` on the input pair's tail
node -- `xb.x1.tmid` (branch B) at the 1.200 V points and
`xc.x1.tmid`/`xb.x1.tmid` at the 1.080 V points -- 6-11 ns into the
initialization window, with the initial transient solution itself correct
(pins at the full differential, outputs LOW, StrongARM reset). The named
nodes are on exactly the branches whose pins were driven (branch A, whose
input network is 1 kOhm and was not pin-driven, is never named), including
at the two clean-control points whose DC solve converges untouched without
the supplement.

The source current flows from pin to ground. When the pin is below its
source, the expression is positive and removes charge from the pin. When
the pin is above its source, the expression is negative and adds charge.
The engaged drive therefore has a negative incremental conductance of
−0.01 S. The recorded tail-node diagnostic identifies where ngspice
stopped. It does not isolate gate charge as the cause.

The recorded evidence: the requests, bodies, envelopes (all corners
`error`, `timestep_too_small` diagnostics naming the tail node), invocation
and attempt logs are stored under this manifest's names. The second attempt
(`20261126-552-coldinit-v2`, summary
[`20261126-552-coldinit-v2.md`](20261126-552-coldinit-v2.md)) replaces every
behavioural pin drive with a voltage-controlled switch (a linear
two-terminal stamp) bridging each pin to its own ideal source and measures
all four points with its original crossing cards.
