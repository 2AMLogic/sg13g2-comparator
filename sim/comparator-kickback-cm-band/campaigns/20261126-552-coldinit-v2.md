# Strobe-qualified kickback residue at the DR-0004 cold edge corners (issue #552)

The EE review of PR #166 left two cells without a valid measurement: the
#165 decision-gate rejects `vcm-m050` ss/1.200 V/-40 C and `vcm-p050`
ss/1.080 V/-40 C. The #405 probe showed the floating branch received only a
fraction of its programmed differential there, and the #415 forced-DC arm
showed the gate pass without a decision (the failing DC solve parks the
bistable latch in the HIGH basin before the strobe, the floating
differential stays collapsed ~46x). Neither produced a qualified residue at
either edge. This historical v2 run used bench initialization and reported
four passing points under the original rules. Its undirected crossing cards
could accept a falling transition. The table below records the original
classification. Qualification with the corrected rising-edge deck requires
a separately identified run.
Evidence for [PR #166](https://github.com/2AMLogic/sg13g2-comparator/pull/166);
recorded beside -- never merged into -- the `20261011-165` populations (the
44/45 parent populations are byte-unchanged); no ratification and no spec-row
verdict is claimed here. The range and all ratification decisions remain
with DR-0004.

## Method

The bench is the #165 bench plus exactly one declared `init` supplement
block (the whole body diff, `kickback-init.body.spice`): during [0, 25 ns) a
bench-only initialization phase bridges the four floating input pins to
their analytic DC values -- their own ideal sources `v(bsp)`/`v(bsn)`
(1 mV) and `v(csp)`/`v(csn)` (100 mV), the values the 1 GOhm / 1 pF network
would deliver at a converged DC solve -- and switches all three
non-inverting latch outputs to the wrong-polarity LOW rail (0 V, the one
supply-independent rail). Every drive is a voltage-controlled switch (a
linear two-terminal stamp). The single release control `vclti` falls
25.0 -> 25.1 ns (100 ps edge, pulse width 1 s, never returns), so the
temporary drive is released **before the 29 ns baseline** (`bd0`/`cd0`) and
stays released through the whole evaluation window (30 ns strobe, 55 ns
end); the realized control voltage is re-read from every run
(`init_ctl_1n` = 1, `init_ctl_29n` = 0, measured, not assumed). After
release the off resistance is 1 TΩ, which conducts 1.2 pA at 1.2 V.
For a 1 pF input capacitor, the 1 GΩ input path accounts for 2.5×10⁻⁵
time constants over 25 ns. The parallel 1 TΩ path adds 2.5×10⁻⁸.
The stored v2 deck specified `von=0.6 voff=0.4` on ngspice's native
`sw` model. ngspice 47 ignores those parameters. The measured control
shows the pulse completed by 29 ns, but the v2 deck did not implement
the intended 0.6/0.4 V thresholds. The revised deck uses `vt=0.5 vh=0.1`.
The DUT, the
analysis (`tran 5p 60n`) and all tolerances are untouched; no `.options`,
`.nodeset` or `.ic`.

The first fleet attempt (`20261126-552-coldinit`, stored as the negative
result; manifest
[`comparator-kickback-cm-band-20261126-552-coldinit.manifest.json`](../../../manifests/artifacts/comparator-kickback-cm-band-20261126-552-coldinit.manifest.json))
drove the pins with behavioural current sources. Their polarity drew charge
from pins already below their targets. ngspice stopped at a driven
branch's tail node (`xb`/`xc.x1.tmid`) 6–11 ns into initialization at all
four points. Those diagnostics do not establish the cause. V2 replaced the
four sources with switches to each pin's ideal input.

## Original v2 qualification rules

V2 evaluated the following observations before reporting its classifications.

1. **Release**: `|v(clti)` at 1 ns `- 1 V|` and `|v(clti)` at 29 ns`|` <= 1 mV
   (the temporary drive is proven gone before evaluation, from the run's own
   measurements).
2. **Applied stimulus**: the realized branch B and branch C pin
   differentials at the 30 ns strobe are within **+/- 2 %** of the
   programmed 1 mV and 100 mV. Basis: the #415 clean controls delivered
   99.93-99.96 % of the programmed differential with the same 1 GOhm / 1 pF
   network, while the failing solves at the rejects delivered ~2.2 %; the
   tolerance accepts exactly the delivered stimulus (20x the clean-control
   deficit, 40x below the collapse).
3. **Pre-strobe state**: all three supply-normalized outputs at 29 ns
   <= 0.1 (the complement of the 0.9 decision gate) -- the known opposite
   polarity.
4. **Transitions**: the v2 implementation accepted each finite
   `{a,b,c}_t_half` at or after the strobe. The cards used `cross=1`
   and the qualifier imposed no upper time limit.
5. **Gates**: `dout_1k_end`, `dout_float_small_end`, `dout_float_big_end`
   all >= 0.9 at 55 ns.

For a qualified point the original `kick_sigdep_uv` card (#165's own
29 ns / 55 ns samples, `abs((cd1-cd0)-(bd1-bd0))*1e6`) is graded against
the Row 4 Target (<= 100 uV). V2 recorded a discrepancy with the samples as
a report-level problem. It could also apply a grade before corner validity
was final. The revised grader withholds a verdict in both cases.

A local ngspice 47 replay checked the direction independently. The three
regenerated v2 requests and three bodies matched their manifest hashes
before relocating the OSDI paths and adding parallel `rise=1` cards. For
each of the twelve outputs, the first rising crossing matched the original
undirected crossing at the printed precision. All were in [30, 55] ns.
The replay reproduced the four v2 residues at the precision in the table.
It used the v2 switch model. [Review and replay details](https://github.com/2AMLogic/sg13g2-comparator/pull/554).

## Reproduce

Use the original implementation at commit `2eb11a0418f13a56372bf8829836ed0945a0f27a`
to reproduce the archived v2 requests and comparison (from the repository root; the series is
staged under git-ignored scratch so its bytes are stored, not committed):

```bash
S=20261126-552-coldinit-v2
python3 sim/run_kickback_cm_band.py probe-build --series $S --root sim/.work/kbcm-552
for c in vcm-m050 vcm-nom vcm-p050; do
  python3 sim/run_kickback_cm_band.py probe-run --series $S --condition $c --arm init \
    --root sim/.work/kbcm-552 --retry-refused 6 --retry-wait 60
done
python3 sim/run_kickback_cm_band.py probe-compare --series $S --root sim/.work/kbcm-552
```

Offline, from the stored envelopes: fetch the series' bytes through this
run's manifest (`klt artifact get <manifest>#<name> -o <out>`; the names are
the paths under `sim/comparator-kickback-cm-band/campaigns/20261126-552-coldinit-v2/`),
then `python3 sim/run_kickback_cm_band.py probe-compare --series
20261126-552-coldinit-v2 --root <dir-holding-the-series>`; `--check` is the
read-only twin and is what re-verifies the four classifications against the
stored observations.
