# Integration-node capacitance study: negative result (issue #497)

**Phase 2 of [#479](https://github.com/2AMLogic/sg13g2-comparator/issues/479),
Row 2 noise closure. Question: can matched added capacitance at the
input-pair drains (`np`/`nn`, each capacitor node-to-`vss`, equal per side)
narrow the effective decision bandwidth enough to close Row 2 within the
Row 3c delay limit? Answer: no — not at any capacitance this family can
offer.** Run 2026-11-21 against `05f9d31de`+ (`20261121-issue497` campaign);
every grid ran on the batch fleet with `--wait-for-capacity 600`.

The machine-validated tables (chains hash-checked, candidate bindings
verified, valid/expected counts per statistic) are committed as
[`klt-corner-verification/campaigns/20261121-issue497/capacitance.{json,md}`](../klt-corner-verification/campaigns/20261121-issue497/capacitance.md).
This report is the analysis over them.

## Screen: noise vs worst Row 3c (18 points, all valid)

Control: the archived `ib10_sw20lat6` geometry at 10 µA bias, zero added
capacitance. Candidates: `cadd1f` / `cadd2f` / `cadd4f` (structural
candidate netlists, committed under
[`candidates/`](../comparator-integration-capacitance/candidates/)), each
the same geometry plus the declared per-side capacitance. Noise statistic:
mean of the per-point two-rung probit-slope sigma over the 18 screening
points, N = 80 trials per rung, injected density **36.27 nV/√Hz — the
unchanged compliance bench**.

| setting | C per side [fF] | noise σ₁₈ [mV rms] | vs control | worst t_d @ 0.1 mV [ns] | vs control | decision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| control (archived `20261115-issue92-bias`) | 0 | 1.05259 | — | 1.862 @ ss_-40c_1.08v | — | — |
| `cadd1f` | 1 | 1.041 | −1.1 % | 1.880 | +1.0 % | eliminate |
| `cadd2f` | 2 | 1.022 | −2.9 % | 1.899 | +2.0 % | eliminate |
| `cadd4f` | 4 | 1.025 | −2.6 % | 1.936 | +4.0 % | eliminate |

All 18 points valid for every statistic (the grader's independence, probe
and gate checks all passed; per-statistic counts are in `capacitance.md`).

**Why every setting is eliminated.** Reaching 1.0 mV from the control's
18-point mean needs a uniform **5.0 %** sigma reduction (1.0/1.05259 =
0.950). The best observed deficit is 2.9 % (2 fF), and 4 fF does no better
than 2 fF (2.6 %) — the effect saturates. The screen's own sampling floor
is comparable: with ±13 % per-point scatter at N = 80 per rung, an 18-point
mean carries ≈ 3 % standard error, so even the 2 fF deficit is at the floor
(paired per-point deltas vs the archived control: −11.5 µV / −30.8 µV /
−27.7 µV with paired SEs of 29–36 µV, t = −0.4 to −0.9). Extrapolating the
delay axis instead: the ~5 % sigma cut needs ≈ 10 % bandwidth reduction;
at the observed marginal trade (+1 % delay per fF, rising), that is ≥ 5–8 fF,
pushing Row 3c to ≈ 1.99–2.01 ns against the 2.0 ns Target at its already
binding corner (ss, −40 °C, 1.08 V). The family cannot close Row 2 within
Row 3c. **A negative result completes the study: no setting advanced, and
the full-grid Rows 1–5 remain unmeasured for every setting.**

## Phase 1 calibration: the density does not move

[Stored calibration run](20261121-issue497-calibration.md): the loop-broken
front-end model with the capacitance mapped node-to-node (`np` → `aon`,
`nn` → `aop`), at `tt/sf/ff/fs` bounding points, control row reproducing
Phase 1's measured `ib10_sw20lat6` densities to the printed digits. Result:
**1–4 fF changes neither the differential output density at 1 MHz nor the
absolute gain at 1 Hz** — `S = white_out(1 MHz) / |Av(1 Hz)|` is identical
to 10 significant digits across all four rows at all four points (36.78 /
29.72 / 45.61 / 30.52 nV/√Hz at tt/sf/ff/fs). A two-row mechanism control
(0 vs 4 nF) confirms the parameter genuinely applies (`white_out` moves
49.9×, `gain_dc` does not). So this projection, labelled as such, is exact
for this family: **candidate own density = control density**; the screen's
small noise reduction is a pure decision-bandwidth effect at fixed density,
and it saturates. The unchanged-bench comparison at 36.27 nV/√Hz remains
the only compliance-relevant statistic; no Row 2 Target verdict is claimed
(the 18-point screen is not a 45-point verdict).

## What this establishes for #479

Added integration-node capacitance is a dead end for Row 2 closure on this
topology: the noise deficit saturates at ~2–3 % (≈ the screen floor) while
the delay cost grows monotonically toward the Row 3c limit. Combined with
Phase 1's finding that the diode-connected loads carry 85–92 % of the
differential output noise power, the result points away from node loading
and toward the load/width axis — which is exactly the separate PMOS-width
study [#498](https://github.com/2AMLogic/sg13g2-comparator/issues/498).

## Provenance, seeds, tooling

- Screens: 3 regeneration jobs + 9 transient_noise parts (one six-point
  part per process section — the union covers the 18 screening points
  exactly once each, N = 80 per rung; the fivefold duplication a
  request-only build produced is fixed in `build --screen` and guarded by a
  plan-time union check). Seeds are the bench defaults committed in each
  request (20260916). ngspice's TRNOISE generator ignores klt's per-sample
  `.options seed=`
  ([klayout-tools#2963](https://github.com/2AMLogic/klayout-tools/issues/2963)),
  and separate transient sources cannot be independently seeded
  ([klayout-tools#5772](https://github.com/2AMLogic/klayout-tools/issues/5772));
  most samples repeat a raw noise draw also seen at another grid point
  (1264–1368 of 1440 per setting), which widens the grid mean's sampling
  error and is a further reason the screen treats ~3 % differences as its
  floor. Per-point independence (duplicate draws within a point, missing
  raw-noise probes) is enforced by the grader.
- No new generic `klt` capability gap surfaced: the per-process screen
  parts, `corners.params` rows and `--batch-set` all behaved as documented.
- Structural DUT binding: every bench body declares its named candidate
  (name, path, sha256); the grader re-reads the reference file, re-hashes
  it and re-derives it from today's `design/comparator.spice` (issue #497's
  "Extra elements require a structural DUT binding"). The schematic default
  and geometry-only paths are unchanged; `./sim/characterize.sh report
  check` is fresh on the re-minted item 8 report (`...-schematic-r3`).
