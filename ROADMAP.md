# Roadmap

A standalone dynamic latched comparator (single-tail StrongARM, DR-0001) on the
open IHP SG13G2 PDK, built and verified with klayout-tools (`klt`), xschem and
ngspice.

Status as of 2026-11-13, `main` @ `dd316add0`. The verdict of record is the
frozen `klt signoff` report; if this file disagrees with it, the report wins.

The spec is ratified ([DR-0002](spec/decision-records/0002-target-spec-ratification.md));
ratified is not met. On the schematic over the 45-point PVT grid:

- Met at 45/45: offset 3 sigma (whole latch, nominal common mode), decision
  time and metastability, kickback residue.
- **Not met:** kickback charge (39/45 points over 25 fC) and input-referred
  noise on the compliance path (grid mean 1.305 mV rms against 1.0 mV).
- Stretch bounds missed for offset, decision time at `ss`, and power.

Items 5 and 8 cannot turn `met` by adding evidence: the design misses two
Targets, so only a design change or a new decision record moves them. The
sizing study (PR #121, [#92](https://github.com/2AMLogic/sg13g2-comparator/issues/92))
found an input pair (8u/0.34u) that closes kickback charge and no candidate
that closes noise (1.27 to 1.34 mV grid mean).

Post-layout state, from [`sim/comparator-pex/`](sim/comparator-pex/README.md):

- Extracted kickback grid: 45/45 corners measured on both sides
  (`reports/batch-extracted-grid.kickback.20261031.md`).
- Extracted regeneration grid: 42/45; the three `ss`/125 C corners are
  unmeasured (`reports/batch-extracted-grid.regeneration.20261031.md`).
- Extracted whole-latch Monte Carlo: 20 offset and 11 noise points of 45 have
  data; `tt`/-40 C/1.32 V offset is a recorded failed run
  (`reports/mc-rollup.20261103-g.md`). Several points lost shards.
- The grids ran on development `klt` clients, not the pinned release; none of
  this is a DR-0002 verdict.

Common-mode range: no row is ratified. The draft DR-0004 (PR
[#166](https://github.com/2AMLogic/sg13g2-comparator/pull/166)) proposes a
±50 mV window. Since then the offset band was completed as a labelled
supplement (#89; the parent campaign's 43/45 is unchanged) and the two
kickback-residue rejects were probed (#405): the evidence favours a bench
DC-operating-point artifact over latch marginality; the cells stay rejected.
